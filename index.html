Ask a Question
Do I need to live in Chennai to train with you?

Not at all. Online coaching is available worldwide through video calls and personalized workout videos. In-person training is available in Chennai for local clients.

I’ve never worked out before. Can you still help me?

Yes. Every program is built around your current fitness level, whether you’re a complete beginner or already experienced.

What’s included in online coaching?

A personalized workout plan, a custom nutrition plan, and regular check-ins and video calls to track progress and adjust your program.

Do you offer Pilates separately from strength training?

Yes. As a certified Pilates instructor, Naresh offers standalone Pilates sessions or combined programs with strength and mobility work.

How much does coaching cost?

Pricing depends on your goals, current fitness level and coaching mode (online or in-person). Message directly on WhatsApp for a custom quote.

How do I get started?

Message on WhatsApp or Instagram for a free initial conversation about your goals, and Naresh will guide you on the right plan from there.
